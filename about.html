Our Story

We threw parties, then watched the best shots vanish into someone else’s camera roll. PXI exists so that never happens again.

It began with a group of international friends who loved bringing people together. We spent years hosting and learning what makes a night memorable, until the logistics crowded out the joy and we stepped back.

In our own events, another problem surfaced: moments scattered across dozens of camera rolls. Months later, someone would uncover a buried photo—the perfect angle of you—and that rediscovery felt electric.

That spark became PXI. We set out to build a shared album so no memory gets lost, which grew into PXIStudio—a live, organized camera roll for real life that removes friction and keeps every angle together.

We then bridged digital to physical with PXIClip, so favorite frames jump from phone to print in seconds—memories meant to be kept, not buried in chats.
