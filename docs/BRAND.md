# PXI brand: voice for video

PXI sells a habit change, not a photo app. Uber changed how you get a cab and Instagram changed what a camera is for. PXI changes what happens to the night after it ends.

## The pain: "send me the pics" hell

- You buy the ticket, and then **you are the camera**.
- You wake up with 200 photos of your friends and 0 of you.
- The best photo of you is on a stranger's phone, and you'll never see it.
- Three days of "send me the pics??", "who has the one from the stairs", "airdrop it", "it won't send".
- The group chat goes quiet and the night quietly disappears.

## The new habit

Buy the ticket, live the night together, wake up to every photo you're in. **The night belongs to everyone.**

Our core narrative has three beats, and every video tells it in this order:

1. **The ticket.** The night starts here, the moment you're in.
2. **The camera.** Everyone shoots, and nobody has to be the one who shoots.
3. **The morning after.** Every photo you're in, already waiting for you.

Hero line: *"The ticket, / the camera, / the morning after. / Buy the ticket, shoot the night together, and wake up to every photo you're in."*

## Rules

- **Concrete nouns over adjectives.** Ticket, camera roll, stairs, 3am, group chat, the flash. Never "amazing experience".
- **Three-beat rhythm.** Lines come in threes. Comma, comma, full stop.
- **Lowercase is allowed** and is the default on screen. Fragments are fine.
- **Sell the lifestyle, not the features.** Say what the morning feels like, not what the app does.
- **No hype punctuation.** No exclamation marks and no emoji in on-screen type.
- **Say "you" and "everyone"**, never "users" or "attendees".
- **We never film ourselves.** No founders to camera, no actors, no phone held up to the lens. The only footage is footage from real nights, and it only appears as a texture on a 3D object (a print, a ticket).

## Banned words

`revolutionary` · `seamless` · `unlock` · `elevate` · `platform` · `solution`

Also avoid, because they read as ad copy: `game-changer`, `next-level`, `effortless`, `experience` (as a noun for a night), `memories` (say "photos" or "the night"), `AI-powered`, `discover`, `journey`, `curated`.

## Good vs bad

| Bad | Good | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Unlock seamless photo sharing at every event. | wake up to every photo you're in. | Banned words, and it names a feature instead of a morning. |
| The revolutionary platform for nightlife memories. | the ticket, the camera, the morning after. | Three concrete nouns tell the whole story. |
| Never miss a moment again! | 200 photos of your friends. 0 of you. | A specific, felt pain beats a promise. |
| Elevate your night out with AI-powered galleries. | everyone shoots. nobody begs for pics. | The benefit is social, not technical. |
| Share your experience with fellow attendees. | the night belongs to everyone. | "You" and "everyone", never "attendees". |
| Download PXI today! | buy the ticket. | The CTA is the habit, three words, no exclamation mark. |

## Headline sets for the templates

Every template takes `headlines: [string, string, string]`, one per beat. Some sets that follow the rules:

- `the ticket,` / `the camera,` / `the morning after.`
- `200 photos.` / `0 of you.` / `until now.`
- `you bought the ticket.` / `everyone shot the night.` / `you're in every photo.`
- `no more` / `"send me the pics??"` / `it's already there.`
- `doors at 10.` / `flash at 1.` / `photos by 9.`
